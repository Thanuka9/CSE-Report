# Review guide

For each exception, verify:

1. Correct issuer and filing version/hash.
2. Company or Bank scope, not Group/Consolidated.
3. Correct standalone-quarter or as-at column.
4. Raw label, sign and numeric value.
5. Currency and scale evidence nearest to the value.
6. Metric mapping and explicit three-month evidence for every Q4 flow value.
7. Security class for quarter-end price, especially voting versus non-voting symbols.
8. Assets, liabilities and equity reconciliation and any derived-liability lineage.

Record a decision reason. Corrected curated facts do not alter the raw extraction record.
